Printing tips

Paper

Letter size, portrait. Heavier paper (65 lb cardstock or more) holds the colors best and hangs flat.

Settings

Print at actual size, or "fit to page," with borderless printing if your printer has it. Print double-sided to make a booklet.

Hanging it

Staple or bind along the top edge, then punch a hole above the art. A local print shop can bind it for a few dollars.

Sharing

Print as many as you like for family, friends and classrooms. Please don't sell it, and send people to numunuu.com/calendar for their own copy.
