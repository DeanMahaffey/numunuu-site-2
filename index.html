Still here

The Comanche Nation is headquartered in Lawton, Oklahoma, with about 17,000 enrolled citizens. The history on this site belongs to living people, and the language is still spoken and taught.

Visit the museum in Lawton, learn a few words, or send a correction. This site gets better when Comanche people help shape it.
