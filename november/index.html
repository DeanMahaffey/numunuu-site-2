Native American Heritage Month · November

30 Days of Comanche

One Comanche story for every day of November: a person, a moment, an object, or a place. A new one opens each day at midnight, Lawton time.

All 30 days

Open any day that has been released. Locked days open on their date.

About these storiesEvery day is drawn from the sources listed on the linked exhibit pages and on About and sources. Dates follow the record, and where the record disagrees, we say so. Corrections from Comanche families and knowledge-keepers are welcome.
