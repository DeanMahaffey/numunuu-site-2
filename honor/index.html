Comanche veterans

Honor wall

Comanche men and women have served in the United States armed forces, in war and in peace, for more than a century. This wall starts with the seventeen code talkers of World War II, and grows with the names families send.

Honor someone in your family

If someone in your family is a Comanche veteran, living or passed on, you can send their name for the wall. Every name is read by a person before it goes up.

Please only send a name you have the family's blessing to share. Nothing is published without it, and you can ask for a name to be changed or taken down at any time.

This wall honors service. It is an independent project of numunuu.com and is not an official record of the Comanche Nation or the U.S. military.
